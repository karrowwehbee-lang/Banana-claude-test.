# Design standards & technical reference

Typical professional values for residential interiors. They are guidance, not code:
always check local building regulations and the client's specific needs. Values are
in millimetres unless stated.

## Circulation & clearances

| Situation | Minimum | KAÏRA target |
|---|---|---|
| Main circulation path | 900 | 1100–1200 |
| Secondary path between furniture | 600 | 750–900 |
| Sofa edge to coffee table | 400 | 450 |
| Behind dining chair to wall (seated) | 900 | 1000–1100 |
| Behind dining chair with walkway | 1100 | 1200 |
| Kitchen work aisle (one cook) | 1000 | 1100–1200 |
| Kitchen aisle (two cooks) / around island | 1200 | 1200–1400 |
| Side and foot of bed | 600 | 750–900 |
| In front of wardrobe (doors open) | 900 | 1000 |
| In front of WC | 600 | 800 |
| WC centreline to side wall/fixture | 400 | 450 |
| In front of vanity | 700 | 900 |
| Desk chair zone behind desk | 900 | 1100 |

## Furniture dimensions

### Living
- Sofa: seat height 400–450, seat depth 550–650, overall depth 900–1100, arm height 550–650.
- Lounge chair: seat height 380–420, overall 750–900 W × 800–950 D.
- Coffee table: height 300–420 (≈ sofa seat height minus 30–80); length ≈ 2/3 of sofa.
- Side table: height within 50 of the sofa arm.
- Media unit: depth 400–500; TV centre at seated eye height ≈ 1000–1100 from floor.
- Rug: front legs of all seating on the rug; leave 200–450 of floor at room edges.

### Dining
- Table height 730–760; allow 600 width per seat (700 for luxury comfort).
- Table depth 900–1000 (1100+ for a generous centrepiece setting).
- Round table: Ø1200 seats 4–5, Ø1500 seats 6–7, Ø1800 seats 8.
- Pendant bottom 750–850 above the table top; fixture width ≈ 1/2–2/3 of table width.

### Kitchen
- Worktop height 900 (adjust 850–950 to the client's height); depth 600–650.
- Upper cabinets start 500–600 above the worktop; depth 330–350.
- Island: minimum 1200 × 600 worktop; seating overhang 250–300.
- Counter stool seat 650 for a 900 worktop; bar stool seat 750 for 1050 bar height.
- Allow 600 seating width per stool.
- Tall units: 2100–2400, or full height to ceiling with a 5–10 mm shadow gap.

### Bedroom
- Mattress sizes (W × L): EU 160 × 200, 180 × 200, 200 × 200; US Queen 1520 × 2030,
  US King 1930 × 2030, California King 1830 × 2130.
- Bed top of mattress 550–650 from floor; headboard 1100–1400 high (taller for drama).
- Nightstand height within ±50 of the mattress top; width 450–600.
- Wardrobe internal depth 600; long hanging 1600–1800; double hanging 1000 per rail;
  shelving pitch 300–350; drawers 150–250 high.

### Bathroom
- Vanity height 850–900; basin top for vessel basins 850–900 to rim.
- Mirror centre ≈ 1600 from floor; vanity lighting at face height on both sides
  where possible.
- Shower: minimum 900 × 900; KAÏRA target 1200 × 900 or larger, walk-in with
  ≥ 800 opening; floor falls 1–2% towards the drain.
- Freestanding bath: ≥ 150 to the wall on each side (300+ to clean and to read as sculpture).
- Wet zones need waterproofing, slip-resistant floor finishes (check R10/R11 or
  equivalent DCOF rating) and correct IP-rated fittings.

### Office
- Desk height 720–750 (or sit-stand 650–1250); depth 700–800; width ≥ 1400.
- Screen top at or slightly below eye level; viewing distance 500–750.
- Bookshelves depth 300–400; shelf span ≤ 900 for 19–25 mm timber without deflection
  (reinforce or use steel for longer spans).

## Lighting

| Space | Ambient (lux) | Task (lux) | Colour temp |
|---|---|---|---|
| Living | 100–200 | 300 reading | 2700K |
| Dining | 150–200 | — | 2700K |
| Kitchen | 200–300 | 500 on worktop | 3000K |
| Bedroom | 100–150 | 300 reading | 2700K |
| Bathroom | 200 | 300–500 at mirror | 3000K |
| Office | 300 | 500 on desk | 3000–4000K |

- Always CRI ≥ 90 (≥ 95 for galleries, kitchens and dressing areas).
- Use dim-to-warm or tunable white where the budget allows.
- Downlights: use them sparingly, with a deep recess and low glare (UGR < 19), aimed
  at walls and objects rather than in a grid.
- Integrated linear LED in joinery: 2700–3000K, diffused profile, no visible dots.
- Plan at least 3 scenes per main room (e.g. Day, Evening, Ambience).

## Materials quick reference

| Material | Best for | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Marble (Calacatta, Statuario, Arabescato) | Feature walls, vanities, tables | Etching from acids; seal; honed hides wear |
| Quartzite (Taj Mahal, Fusion) | Kitchen worktops | Verify it is true quartzite (hardness) |
| Travertine | Floors, sculptural pieces | Fill or leave open; seal in wet areas |
| Sintered stone / porcelain slab | Kitchens, outdoor, wet areas | Edge detailing; mitred edges only |
| Engineered oak (smoked, fumed, natural) | Floors, joinery | Board width 180–300 for luxury feel |
| Walnut veneer | Joinery, panelling | Bookmatch or slip-match; UV darkening |
| Lime plaster / tadelakt / microcement | Walls, bathrooms | Applicator skill; microcement needs a stable substrate |
| Bouclé, mohair, velvet, linen | Upholstery | Martindale ≥ 30 000 for main seating |
| Leather (aniline, semi-aniline) | Seating, wrapped details | Aniline marks easily |
| Brushed brass, bronze, blackened steel | Hardware, trims, frames | Specify lacquered or living finish |

## Custom furniture technical sheet

Use this format for any custom piece:

```
## [Piece name] — KAÏRA Custom
Concept: one-line design intent.

Overall dimensions: W ___ × D ___ × H ___ mm
Key sub-dimensions: seat height, top thickness, plinth/recess, overhangs, clearances

Materials & finishes
- Carcass: …
- Visible surfaces: … (species, cut, finish, sheen)
- Metal: … (alloy, finish, lacquer)
- Upholstery: … (fabric, Martindale, foam density, fill)

Construction
- Joinery method (mitre, dovetail, domino, concealed fixings)
- Structure / reinforcement, max span and load
- Hardware (hinges, runners, push-to-open, soft close)
- Integrated services (LED, cable management, ventilation)

Drawings required: plan, front and side elevations, section, 1:1 details
Tolerances: ±1 mm joinery, ±2 mm overall (adjust to the workshop)
Notes for the workshop: grain direction, edge profile, shadow gaps, finish samples
```

Check proportion: favour thin visual edges (12–20 mm tops, slim legs), deep shadow
gaps, and avoid bulky plinths unless intentional.
