# Client documents

All client-facing documents use the KAÏRA voice: refined, warm, precise; short
paragraphs; no clichés, no emoji, no exaggerated claims. Leave clearly marked
placeholders (`[…]`) for any information you do not have — never invent client
details, prices, dates or legal terms.

## Project proposal

```
KAÏRA Interior Design
Design Proposal — [Project name]
Prepared for [Client] · [Date] · Ref. [KAIRA-YYYY-###]

1. Introduction
   A short, personal opening: the opportunity and our understanding of the client.

2. Understanding of the brief
   Site, scope, client aspirations, constraints.

3. Design vision
   Concept name, narrative, mood words, key design principles.

4. Scope of services
   Phase 1 — Concept design (survey, brief, concept, moodboards, initial layouts)
   Phase 2 — Design development (detailed layouts, materials, lighting, furniture, 3D)
   Phase 3 — Technical design (drawings, specifications, joinery details, schedules)
   Phase 4 — Procurement & FF&E (sourcing, orders, tracking)
   Phase 5 — Site supervision & installation (visits, styling, handover)

5. Deliverables
   List per phase.

6. Timeline
   Phase · duration · key milestones (estimate; subject to approvals and suppliers).

7. Fees
   Fee structure (fixed fee, % of construction cost, or hourly) · payment schedule ·
   what is excluded (consultants, permits, furniture and construction costs, travel).

8. Why KAÏRA
   2–3 sentences on approach and standards.

9. Next steps & acceptance
   Validity of the proposal, signature block.
```

## Project description (portfolio, website, press)

Length 120–250 words. Structure:
1. Opening line that captures the spirit of the project.
2. Context: location, type of residence, client (anonymous unless approved).
3. The design idea and how it shapes the space.
4. Materials, light and signature details.
5. Closing line on the experience of living in it.

Also offer: a 1-line caption, a 40-word social media version, and 5–8 relevant hashtags
if asked.

## Client presentation

Outline for a design presentation deck (10–16 slides):

1. Cover — project name, KAÏRA logo, date
2. The brief — client goals in their own words
3. Site analysis — plan, light, constraints
4. Concept — name, narrative, mood words
5. Moodboard
6. Color palette
7. Materials & finishes board
8. Layout plan(s)
9. Lighting concept
10. Key furniture & custom pieces
11. 3D renders (one per slide, full bleed)
12. Details & signature moments
13. Budget overview (if requested)
14. Timeline & next steps
15. Thank you / contact

Design rules: generous white space, one idea per slide, large imagery, a refined
serif for titles with a clean sans-serif for text, palette drawn from the project.
For each slide, give the title, the key message, the visual and short speaker notes.
