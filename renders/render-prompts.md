# Série Minérale — Living Room — Render Prompts

KAÏRA Interior Design · Appartement Quai de Béthune, Paris

Four photorealistic views of one project. Materials, palette and furniture stay identical across all views so the set reads as one scheme.

## Project reference (from the concept)

- **Room:** 6.5 m × 5.0 m, ceiling 3.0 m, Haussmannian volume with restored cornices and mouldings. Tall south-west window wall along the 6.5 m side. Fireplace on the 5.0 m east wall. Walnut credenza in a niche on the wall opposite the windows.
- **Floor:** French oak point de Hongrie (chevron) parquet, 120 × 600 mm blocks, matte hardwax oil.
- **Walls:** Roman lime plaster (calce rasata), soft-troweled matte, Chalked Lime `#EBE7DF`.
- **Fireplace:** leathered Taj Mahal quartzite, 30 mm slabs with mitred 45° edges. The surround extends sideways into a low stone ledge 450 mm high.
- **Joinery:** smoked American black walnut, matte. Flush motorized panels hide the TV.
- **Metal:** hand-brushed patinated bronze, Aged Bronze `#675745`, with a continuous 10 mm shadow gap at the baseboards and joinery junctions.
- **Furniture:**
  - Modular low sofa, 3600 × 1100 × 660 mm, off-white bouclé on a recessed smoked oak plinth.
  - Pair of sculptural armchairs, 850 × 880 × 720 mm, oxblood mohair velvet (`#4A151B`) on patinated bronze frames, angled at 45°.
  - Monolithic coffee table, 1600 × 800 × 320 mm, honed Navona travertine, 420 mm from the sofa.
  - Window daybed, 1950 × 750 × 380 mm, cognac aniline leather on blackened steel legs.
  - Low credenza, 2400 × 480 × 520 mm, smoked walnut with a leathered Taj Mahal quartzite top.
- **Lighting:**
  - Hidden cove light behind the cornice (2700K).
  - Trimless downlights (2700K, 15° beam).
  - Linear grazers on the stone and shelving.
  - Patinated bronze and frosted hand-blown glass pendant over the seating.
  - Bronze floor lamp with a silk shade by an armchair.
- **Textiles and art:**
  - Sheer Belgian linen drapery on recessed tracks.
  - Ink blue (`#1C2536`) accents in cushions and rug.
  - Large textured white gesso canvas, 1800 × 2200 mm.

**Fixes to the concept:**
- The concept named the shadow gaps bronze in one section and brass in another. These prompts use bronze to match the materials table.
- The floor is shown as point de Hongrie (chevron), as specified, not herringbone.
- The credenza top is quartzite, not "quartz".

**Choice made for these prompts:** the concept does not say where the gesso canvas hangs. These prompts place it above the credenza.

## Using these prompts

- **Midjourney:** paste the prompt and add the ending shown under it.
- **Stable Diffusion / Flux:** use the prompt with this negative prompt:
  `distorted perspective, warped lines, cartoon, CGI look, oversaturated, low detail, clutter, text, watermark, people`
- **Gemini or ChatGPT image:** use the prompt as written. For edits, describe only what changes and add "keep everything else identical".
- **D5, V-Ray, Enscape, Lumion, Corona:** use the Camera and Lighting notes as scene settings:
  - Views 01 and 03: daylight white balance about 5500–6500K.
  - View 04: blue-hour sky with 2700K interior lights.
  - Post-production: subtle bloom, vignetting under 10%.
