# KAÏRA Interior Design — logo & visual identity

Concept: **the shadow gap** — the fine recessed joint between two materials in KAÏRA interiors becomes the signature
of the custom wordmark (floating arm of the K, floating bar of the A, free leg of the R, lozenge dots on the Ï).
Monogram: K and A sharing one stroke, set in an open arch (the doorway).
