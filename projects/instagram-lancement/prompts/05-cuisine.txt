A high-end contemporary kitchen, square composition. A monolithic island clad in polished Calacatta Viola marble with dramatic bordeaux veining, bookmatched on the front, with a thin 2 cm mitred worktop edge. Tall floor-to-ceiling cabinetry in dark smoked walnut with handle-less flush doors and fine shadow gaps, an integrated tall niche in brushed bronze. Warm white walls and ceiling, a slim linear pendant in brushed bronze above the island, three walnut stools. Wide-plank natural oak floor. Soft daylight from a large window on the side, a ceramic bowl of lemons and a cutting board on the island. 24 mm lens, eye level, slightly off-centre view.
