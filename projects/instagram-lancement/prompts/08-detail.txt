Square image. An architectural detail close-up, abstract and minimal.
Subject: the vertical junction where a wall of honed travertine meets a smoked walnut panel, separated by a precise 10 mm shadow gap; a slender brushed bronze profile caps the panel edge; a fine line of warm indirect light grazes the stone from above.
Detail: extreme micro-texture of the travertine pores and of the walnut grain, the bronze showing fine brushing marks.
Camera: 85 mm lens, slightly oblique, shallow depth of field with the junction in sharp focus and the stone softly falling out of focus.
Light: soft raking light, deep calm shadows, quiet and sculptural.
