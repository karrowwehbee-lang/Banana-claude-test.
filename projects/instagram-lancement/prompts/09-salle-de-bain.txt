An elegant contemporary master bathroom, square composition. Walls and floor in large-format honed travertine with continuous veining, a sculptural freestanding bathtub carved from solid pale stone in the centre. A recessed wall niche lined in polished olive green marble (Verde Alpi style), lit from within. Brushed bronze wall-mounted tapware. A floating double vanity in smoked walnut with a stone basin, a round mirror with a thin bronze frame. Soft daylight from a tall window with a sheer linen blind, gentle reflections on the stone. A folded ecru towel and a single stem in a glass vase. 24 mm lens, eye level.
