A luxurious contemporary living room in a villa, square composition. Large open volume with a ceiling height of about 3.2 m. One full wall clad in large-format honed travertine slabs with a slim shadow gap at the floor and ceiling. Floor-to-ceiling walnut joinery with flush doors and a lit niche. A low, deep sofa in ivory bouclé facing a sculptural oval coffee table in solid travertine, one lounge chair in olive green velvet as the single accent. Wide-plank natural oak floor, a hand-tufted wool rug in warm white. Tall linen sheers filtering soft afternoon sun from a floor-to-ceiling window on the left, casting long gentle shadows. A brushed bronze floor lamp, a ceramic vase with one olive branch, a few art books. 24 mm lens, eye level, wide view.
