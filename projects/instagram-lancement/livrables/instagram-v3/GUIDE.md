## Posting order

1. Publish POST-01, then POST-02, … up to POST-12, in this exact order (same day, a few minutes apart, is ideal).
2. For each post: tap **+** ▸ **Post** ▸ select multiple ▸ tap the files **in the order 1, 2, 3…** ▸ choose the **4:5 (portrait)** crop icon so nothing is cut ▸ Next ▸ paste `caption+hashtags.txt` ▸ Share.
3. When the 12 are live, pin in this order: **POST-02 Our Services**, then **POST-01 Meet KAÏRA**, then **POST-03 The Project** (open the post ▸ ••• ▸ Pin to your profile). Instagram shows the last pinned post first, so the top row reads: The Project · Meet KAÏRA · Our Services. If the order differs, unpin and pin again.

Final profile: `apercu-profil.jpg`.

### POST-09 · Design notes — 5 keys to a calm interior

**Caption**

5 keys to a calm interior. Save this post for your next project.

01 · Start with light: plan daylight first, then add warm, indirect layers.
02 · Limit the palette: three calm base tones and a single accent.
03 · Choose honest materials: stone, solid wood, linen and wool age beautifully.
04 · Hide what you don't need to see: flush doors, integrated storage, concealed lighting.
05 · Give art a wall of its own: one strong piece, well lit, with space around it.
