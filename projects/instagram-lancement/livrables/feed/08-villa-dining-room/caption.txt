Villa — Dining room.

Receiving under a line of light. A long pale stone table on a fluted dark base, eight chairs in bouclé and dark wood, and a sculptural pendant that draws a free line of light above. A deep bordeaux artwork gives the room its character.

Swipe for the asymmetric wood library and the veined marble column that mark the passage towards the living room.
