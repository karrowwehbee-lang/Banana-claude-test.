Villa — Living room.

A reception room composed around art. Cream wall panels with fine shadow gaps frame a large line drawing, while vertical wood columns with integrated light give the room its rhythm and height. Two low modular sofas, in deep taupe leather and soft cream, face each other across a stone and dark-wood coffee table.

A continuous cove of warm light traces the ceiling and ties the whole space together.

Swipe for the wood columns and the media wall.
