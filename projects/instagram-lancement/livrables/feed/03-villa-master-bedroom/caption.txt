Villa — Master bedroom.

A suite built around a single gesture: a band of dark veined marble, backlit, rising behind the bed. Around it, cream panels, fluted wood columns with hidden light and a wraparound headboard with soft, rounded lines.

Olive bed linen, a channel-tufted bench on a brushed-gold base and glass globe pendants complete a quiet, enveloping atmosphere.
