Villa — Guest bedroom.

Softness and light. A low upholstered platform bed with rounded corners, a band of vertical wood slats, opal globe pendants and slim black-metal side tables.

Opposite the bed, a white veined marble wall frames the media unit and a floating dressing table in wood and marble.
