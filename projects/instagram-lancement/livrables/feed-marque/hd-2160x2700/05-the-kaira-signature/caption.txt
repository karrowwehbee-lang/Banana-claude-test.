Four constants in every KAÏRA project.

01 · Layered light: daylight first, then hidden lines of warm light in ceilings, niches and joinery.
02 · Stone & wood: veined marbles, natural oak and dark woods, chosen to age with grace.
03 · Bespoke joinery: libraries, wall panels and furniture drawn for one place only.
04 · Art at the heart: every room is composed around one strong piece.
