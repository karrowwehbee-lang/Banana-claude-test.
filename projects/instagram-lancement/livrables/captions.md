## 02 · Villa — Living room

Villa — Living room.

A reception room composed around art. Cream wall panels with fine shadow gaps frame a large line drawing, while vertical wood columns with integrated light give the room its rhythm and height. Two low modular sofas, in deep taupe leather and soft cream, face each other across a stone and dark-wood coffee table.

A continuous cove of warm light traces the ceiling and ties the whole space together.

Swipe for the wood columns and the media wall.

## 03 · Villa — Master bedroom

Villa — Master bedroom.

A suite built around a single gesture: a band of dark veined marble, backlit, rising behind the bed. Around it, cream panels, fluted wood columns with hidden light and a wraparound headboard with soft, rounded lines.

Olive bed linen, a channel-tufted bench on a brushed-gold base and glass globe pendants complete a quiet, enveloping atmosphere.

#KAIRAInteriorDesign #MasterBedroom #BedroomDesign #LuxuryBedroom #MarbleWall #VillaInterior #QuietLuxury #InteriorArchitecture

Villa — Guest bedroom.

## 08 · Villa — Dining room

Villa — Dining room.
