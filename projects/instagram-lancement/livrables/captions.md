## 02 · Villa — Living room

Villa — Living room.

A reception room composed around art. Cream wall panels with fine shadow gaps frame a large line drawing, while vertical wood columns with integrated light give the room its rhythm and height. Two low modular sofas, in deep taupe leather and soft cream, face each other on a pale rug.

A continuous cove of warm light traces the ceiling and ties the whole space together.

## 03 · Villa — Master bedroom

Villa — Master bedroom.

A suite built around a single gesture: a band of dark veined marble, backlit, rising behind the bed. Around it, cream panels, fluted wood columns with hidden light and a wraparound headboard with soft, rounded lines.

Olive bed linen, a channel-tufted bench on a brushed-gold base and glass globe pendants complete a quiet, enveloping atmosphere.

#KAIRAInteriorDesign #MasterBedroom #BedroomDesign #LuxuryBedroom #MarbleWall #VillaInterior #QuietLuxury #InteriorArchitecture

## 05 · Our approach — "Three words, one intention"

Our approach in three words.

Proportion: volumes drawn to human scale, generous circulation, sightlines that breathe.
Material: stone, wood and textiles chosen for their truth and the way they age.
Light: daylight first, then warm layers of indirect light that shape the room.

#KAIRAInteriorDesign #DesignPhilosophy #InteriorArchitecture #Proportion #NaturalMaterials #LightingDesign #InteriorDesign #QuietLuxury
