Around the table.

A boardroom designed for long sessions: one long table with built-in microphones and a planted centre line, a slatted ceiling with linear lights, walnut walls and a wall of green marble that anchors the room. Storage is built in under the windows and into lit shelving.

Interior design & 3D visualization by KAÏRA Interior Design.
